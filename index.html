our mission

build the AI operating layer for the next generation of pasture-based agriculture

factory feedlots scale by eliminating decisions. we're building the tools that let pasture-based farms scale by automating them — so regenerative operations can compete at industrial scale without sacrificing the biology that makes them better.

the problem

farming data is fragmented and dumb

satellite imagery in one place. weather in another. grazing history in a notebook. soil data in a PDF. no AI can help you if it can't access what you know — and right now, none of it talks to each other.

the solution

an intelligent data layer for your whole operation

openpasture cleans, organizes, and connects your farm data into one opinionated system — then combines it with knowledge bases from leading grazing pioneers and API integrations so any AI agent can take autonomous action on your behalf.

how it works
01

ingest

satellite, weather, soil, photos, and vendor telemetry flow through a pipeline that cleans and organizes your farm data automatically.

02

reason

AI agents combine your operational data with historical knowledge from leading grazing pioneers to generate recommendations with full reasoning chains.

03

act

from daily move recommendations to automated rotation scheduling — agents take as many autonomous actions as you allow, up to fully managing herd movement.

intelligent data pipeline

all your farm data, cleaned and connected

satellite imagery, weather, soil surveys, your own observations, vendor telemetry — openpasture ingests it all, cleans it, and organizes it into a structured system that any AI can understand and act on.

  • automatic ingestion from satellite, weather, and soil APIs
  • farmer observations via photo, voice, or text
  • open connectors for vendor hardware and telemetry

curated knowledge base

the wisdom of the best graziers, built in

openpasture encodes operational knowledge from leading regenerative grazing pioneers — not as rigid rules, but as frameworks your AI agent can reference when making decisions about your specific land and conditions.

use any AI agent

your favorite AI, trained on your farm

openpasture isn't locked to one AI. it's the portable knowledge layer that makes any capable agent — Claude, ChatGPT, or whatever comes next — an expert on your specific operation. use the agent you trust most.

  • MCP, API, and CLI connectors for any agent surface
  • same farm intelligence, regardless of which AI you choose
  • switch agents anytime — your data and knowledge travel with you
where we're headed

today: agent-kit + cloud. tomorrow: the full stack.

we're building in the open. the agent is OSS. the cloud platform handles infrastructure. and we're open to building hardware when the system earns it.

now

open-source agent

the core farm reasoning engine — tools, skills, knowledge bases, and data connectors. Apache 2.0. run it locally or connect it to any agent surface.

now

hosted cloud

managed infrastructure, authentication, data pipelines, and the daily brief UI. the easiest way to get started without self-hosting anything.

future

hardware + autonomy

sensor networks, automated gate systems, and full autonomous rotation. when the data layer and trust are proven, we close the loop from recommendation to physical action.
