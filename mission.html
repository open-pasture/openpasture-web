every human deserves to eat animals raised on pasture. every animal deserves to live on grass.

we build tools that help livestock farmers scale their pasture-based operations — so more animals live on grass and more people have access to the food they deserve.

the problem

pasture-based farmers manage some of the most complex operations in agriculture. rotational grazing, herd health, forage recovery, weather — the decisions are constant and the margin for error is thin.

most ag-tech ignores these farmers entirely. the tools that exist are built for confinement operations or row crops. pasture farmers are left with notebooks, spreadsheets, and memory.

what we're building

openpasture is a tools company. today, we build a connective operating layer that integrates through AI agents — giving farmers access to their farm state, practitioner knowledge, and decision support through ChatGPT, Claude, or any MCP-compatible client.

tomorrow, that may include GPS collars, on-farm communications infrastructure, and other hardware to automate and augment as much of the farmer's decision-making and labor as possible. the mission doesn't stop at software.

open source at the core

the agent kit is AGPL-3.0 licensed. the tools, skills, and practitioner knowledge that power openpasture are open and available to any farmer, developer, or organization that wants to contribute or self-host. we believe the infrastructure that helps farmers should belong to the community it serves.
