02 type

Typography

JetBrains Mono makes the product feel operational and tool-like. Libre Baskerville appears in hero and thesis moments to add editorial gravity.

JetBrains Mono weights

400 regular: daily movement plan

500 medium: pasture recovery status

600 semibold: observation request

700 bold: approve movement

800 extra-bold: openpasture

Libre Baskerville display

what a trained farmer would do.

every morning. every paddock.

Root scale

13px base, 14px at lg, 15px at xl and above.

Copy rhythm

Small dense UI type: text-xs, text-sm, and text-[10px].

Voice markers

Lowercase nav, CLI commands, function headings, and bracketed checks.

13 voice

Copy Style Guide

The voice is practical, terse, and command-line flavored. It should sound like a tool a farmer can trust, not a glossy SaaS brochure.

Use

  • [x]lowercase navigation: how-it-works, get-started
  • [x]CLI CTAs: ./demo --interactive, cat README.md
  • [x]function headers: how_it_works()
  • [x]status badges: available-now, early-access-beta

Avoid

  • --generic SaaS claims without operational context.
  • --rounded, soft, lifestyle-forward language.
  • --too many title-case headings in marketing navigation.
  • --CTAs that hide the action behind vague language.
